01 — The Idea

Why jade

玉，石之美者

In Chinese tradition jade is not valued for rarity alone. It is valued because it is worked — and because its qualities were used for two thousand years as the measure of a person's character. That is the idea this group is built on.

It matters to us in two ways. First, how a thing is made: nothing leaves our hands unexamined. Second, how we deal: no hidden flaws, no inflated claims, no vanishing after the invoice is paid. Those are not marketing lines — they are the two things our name is meant to be judged by.

“The gentleman measures his virtue against jade: warm and lustrous, that is benevolence; fine and close-grained, that is wisdom; flawed yet not concealing the beauty, beautiful yet not concealing the flaw — that is loyalty.” The Book of Rites · Meaning of the Diplomatic Mission 《礼记·聘义》

We apply the same idea across five very different businesses — from pearl jewellery to construction machinery. The products have nothing in common. The standard does.

03 — Quality

What we commit to on quality

瑕不掩瑜，瑜不掩瑕，忠也

A flaw disclosed is a specification; a flaw concealed is a breach of contract. These are the four commitments we make on every order, in every division.

01

Nothing is concealed

Where a batch deviates from the agreed specification, we tell you before shipment — with photographs, not adjectives.

02

What is quoted is what is shipped

Material, grade, dimensions and finish are written into the quotation. Substitutions are proposed for approval, never applied silently.

03

Independent inspection is welcome

You may appoint a third-party inspection agency at any stage before shipment. We will arrange access and provide the samples they ask for.

04

Every claim is traceable

Specification sheets, certifications and test reports are issued as documents you can verify — not as badges on a web page.

04 — Process

Four steps, named after jade

《毛传》：治骨曰切，象曰磋，玉曰琢，石曰磨

The classical description of working jade names four operations. We use those four words for the four gates every order passes through.

切

Cutting

Qiē · Incoming inspection

Material and components are checked on arrival. What fails here never enters production.

磋

Filing

Cuō · Machining & assembly

Dimensional accuracy is held to the agreed drawing and recorded batch by batch.

琢

Carving

Zhuó · Precision finishing

Fit, function and appearance are finished to the standard the sample was approved at.

磨

Polishing

Mó · Test, pack & document

Final test, packing against transport damage, and the export documents that follow the goods.
