01 — Channels

Two channels, both monitored

联系方式

Use whichever suits the size of what you are sending. A photograph of a worn part, or a picture of a teapot you want copied, usually travels better than a description — WhatsApp is the faster route for those.

For drawings, specification sheets, packing artwork or anything you would rather not send through a messaging app, use email.

What to include

  • The division — tea ware, jewellery, machinery and parts, electric vehicles, or automotive interior
  • The product — a part number, a model, or the item itself
  • The specification — material, grade, dimensions, finish, and any tolerance that matters
  • The quantity you need now, and what you expect to order again
  • The destination market, so we can confirm documentation, labelling and build requirements
  • Whether it is a stock item or a custom build

Sample and small orders

There is no minimum order. One piece is quoted at wholesale just as a container is, and the unit price moves with the quantity you order — so a sample set, a trial carton or a first small order is a normal starting point. Send the specification and we will quote it at a wholesale price, whatever the volume.

02 — Quoting

Why we quote rather than list prices

报价方式

Freight, duty and the differences between builds move the final number more than the model does. A published price for a teapot or a cargo trike would be wrong for most of the buyers who read it.

01

Against the specification

Material, grade, dimensions and finish are written into the quotation. What is quoted is what is shipped — substitutions are proposed for approval, never applied silently.

02

Against the quantity

A trial quantity by courier and a full container are quoted on the same basis, so you can see both before you commit. If you want a sample first, say so and we will quote it.

03

Against the destination

Your market decides what has to travel with the goods: labelling, packing, inspection and conformity documents. Sending that information with the enquiry saves a round trip.

04

In writing, and traceable

Every claim we make about a product is issued as a document you can verify, not as a badge on a web page — the same rule we apply to our own site.
