01 — What we are

An export partner, not a marketplace

我们做什么

We are not a marketplace and we are not a retail shop. We hold the supplier relationships, we verify what is being shipped, and the goods carry our name. Everything that leaves us passes the same four gates, whether it is a container of excavator undercarriage or a carton of porcelain.

We supply wholesale and OEM / ODM only. Single-piece retail is not available, in any division. That is a deliberate choice rather than a limitation: it keeps the terms we quote to dealers, retailers and own-label buyers clean, and it means the buyer standing next to you in the same market is not paying retail while you pay wholesale.

Our buyers are the people who resell: dealers and rental fleets, jewellers and gift retailers, tea houses and ceremony suppliers, importers assembling a mixed container, and brands building a private-label line.

04 — Where we ship

Markets we already work in

服务市场

We supply into Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Europe, North America and South America. The list is not a claim of local offices — it is the set of destinations we ship to regularly.

What changes between markets is not the product so much as the paperwork and the build: plug and voltage, labelling language, packing standards, and which inspection or conformity documents the destination requires. Tell us the market and we will tell you what the shipment needs — and what we cannot provide, so you are not surprised at the port.
