01 — What is checked

Three points where a defect can be caught

三个关口

Anything caught at the first point costs a phone call. Anything caught at the last point costs a container. That is the whole argument for checking early.

On arrival

Incoming inspection

Material and components are checked against the order before they enter production. Quantity, grade and visible condition are recorded. What fails here never reaches the line — that is the point of doing it first.

During production

Dimensional and finish records

Dimensional accuracy is held to the agreed drawing and recorded batch by batch, so a question raised weeks later is answered from the record rather than from memory. Fit, function and appearance are finished to the standard the sample was approved at.

Before shipment

Final test and packing

Final test, then packing assessed against transport damage rather than against what is cheapest — a part that arrives bent was not a bargain. Documents that follow the goods are prepared at the same stage.

02 — Your own inspector

Independent inspection is welcome

第三方验货

This is the one claim on our site that we would rather you tested than believed.

You may appoint a third-party inspection agency at any stage before shipment. We arrange access and provide the samples they ask for — including the units set aside for the run, not a preparation made for the visit.

Where a batch deviates from the agreed specification, we tell you before shipment, with photographs rather than adjectives. A flaw disclosed is a specification; a flaw concealed is a breach of contract.

How to book one

Tell us the agency, the date and which items they will look at. We will hold the goods and give them access. If you would rather we arranged a routine inspection on your behalf first, ask — it is cheaper before a container than after it.

03 — Documents and limits

What you can verify, and what we will not claim

单证与边界

Specification sheets, test reports and compliance documents are issued as documents you can check, per product and per destination market. Where a market requires a particular conformity document, tell us and we will state whether we can provide it — before the order, not at the port.

What we do not do is publish certification badges on a web page. A logo on a homepage proves nothing about the carton you receive, and we would rather the documents travel with the goods where they can be inspected.

Three commitments that carry the rest

  • What is quoted is what is shipped — material, grade, dimensions and finish are written into the quotation, and substitutions are proposed for approval rather than applied silently.
  • Where the delivered batch differs from the agreed specification, you hear it from us before shipment.
  • Every product claim we make is traceable to a document, not to marketing language.
