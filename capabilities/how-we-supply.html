01 — Flow

Four steps from enquiry to shipment

从询盘到发货
01

Enquiry becomes a specification

We take what you send — a part number, a model, a photograph, a drawing — and write it back to you as a specification. If something is ambiguous, we ask rather than assume.

02

Quotation in writing

Material, grade, dimensions, finish, quantity and the delivery term you want to buy on — quoted against the specification, the quantity and the destination market.

03

Trial before commitment

A sample or trial quantity can be shipped by courier before you commit to a container. Larger and heavier items are better confirmed against a loading plan, so ask us to quote both.

04

Production, packing, documents

Packed against transport damage, loaded against one plan, and documented as a single set that arrives with the goods.

02 — Consolidation

Mixed loads, one loading plan

拼柜与合并

Most buyers do not want a container of one thing. This is the practical reason to buy across the group rather than from five suppliers.

Undercarriage, hydraulic parts, filters and consumables can be combined into one shipment, so a single order covers a service interval rather than one part number. The same applies across divisions: tea ware and jewellery can travel with machinery parts and share the documentation.

Small and light items can be sent as a sample or trial quantity by courier before you commit to a container. Bulk and heavy items are better confirmed against a loading plan, so the freight figure is real rather than indicative.

03 — Terms

Why there is no terms table on this page

交易条款

Payment and delivery terms are agreed in writing for each order and stated on the quotation. We do not publish one table for the group, because the answer genuinely depends on the product, the order size, the destination and whether production is standard or tooled — and a table that is wrong for your order is worse than no table.

Tell us the delivery term you want to buy on and the market the goods are going to, and the terms will come back with the price. If you need the quotation in a particular format for your own paperwork, send the template.

What we do not do

  • Single-piece retail, in any division
  • Silent substitutions of material, grade or finish once a quotation is agreed
  • Certification claims on a web page, in place of documents issued with the goods
