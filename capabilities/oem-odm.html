01 — Two routes

Build to your drawing, or adapt what we run

两种代工方式

Most buyers arrive with one of two situations: they have their own design and want it made, or they have seen something they like and want it changed. Both are ordinary work here.

OEM — built to your specification

Your drawing, your standard

You supply the drawing, the sample or the specification sheet. We build to it, hold the tolerances written on it, and quote against it. Where something on the drawing cannot be met as drawn, we say so before production rather than after.

ODM — adapted from a running build

Start from something that already works

You start from a product we already produce and change what matters for your market: dimensions, motor and controller rating, material, finish, packing. These are quoted on the same basis as a standard order.

Own label and packaging

Your name on the carton

Your label, your packing artwork and your carton markings. Send the artwork and tell us the labelling language the destination market requires, and we will confirm what can be applied at the factory rather than at destination.

02 — What to send

Six things make a custom quote possible

需要提供什么

You do not need all six to start a conversation — a photograph of a worn part or a competitor's product is enough for us to ask the right questions.

  • A drawing, a sample, or a specification sheet — whichever exists
  • Material and grade, where you have a preference or a local requirement
  • Dimensions and tolerances that decide whether the part fits
  • Finish and appearance — the standard the approved sample will be judged against
  • Quantity now, and expected repeat volume — this is what tooling and pricing turn on
  • The destination market, so labelling, packing and documents are planned from the start

If you have no drawing at all, send a photograph with something for scale. It is a normal starting point, not an inconvenience.

03 — Sequence

Sample, then container

先打样，后整柜
01

Specification agreed in writing

Material, grade, dimensions and finish are written down and confirmed before anything is produced or tooling is committed.

02

Sample or trial quantity

Units or parts can be shipped as a sample or trial order before you commit to a container. Small items travel by courier.

03

The approved sample becomes the standard

Once you approve a sample, that approval is the reference the production run is finished to — not a description of it.

04

Production, loading, documents

The run is packed against transport damage and loaded with the rest of your order, so a custom line does not need its own shipment.

04 — Coverage

Which divisions take custom work

适用范围

All five. The route differs by division rather than by willingness:

  • Tea ware — glaze, form, gift-box configuration and branded packaging on ceremony sets and zisha work
  • Jewellery — stone, plating, clasp, length and presentation on pearl lines
  • Machinery and parts — parts matched to a machine model or to a part number, including lines we source to order
  • Electric vehicles — deck size, side-board height, motor and controller rating, axle, suspension and tyre set, voltage for the destination market
  • Automotive interior — interior sets produced to vehicle-specific templates for left- and right-hand-drive markets

Tell us the division and the target market, and we will tell you plainly what can be changed and what cannot.
